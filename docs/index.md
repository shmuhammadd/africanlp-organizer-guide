# Organising AfricaNLP

This guide is for the next person who runs the AfricaNLP workshop. It records what the organisers did for AfricaNLP 2027, the 8th edition, co-located with EACL 2027 in Athens on 9–14 March 2027. Every date, form value and email in it is the one we used. Change the year and the host conference, and most of the work repeats.

The guide follows the order of the work. A first-time general chair can read it front to back in an hour. A chair with one job, such as publication or sponsorship, can go straight to their chapter.

## How the year runs

| When | What happens | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| About 9 months before | Recruit organisers, write and submit the proposal | [Proposal](chapters/proposal) |
| On acceptance | First meeting, assign chairs, set up Drive, email and Slack | [Team](chapters/team), [Infrastructure](chapters/infrastructure) |
| About 2 months before the deadline | First call for papers, website live | [Call for papers](chapters/call-for-papers) |
| About 4 weeks before the deadline | OpenReview venue requested, reviewers invited | [OpenReview](chapters/openreview) |
| Deadline week | Direct submissions close, ARR papers committed | [ARR commitments](chapters/arr-commitments) |
| 3 weeks after the deadline | Reviews in, decisions out | [Reviewing and decisions](chapters/reviewing-and-decisions) |
| 1–2 months before the workshop | Camera-ready, proceedings, programme | [Proceedings](chapters/proceedings), [Programme](chapters/programme) |
| Workshop day | Talks, posters, awards | [Workshop day](chapters/workshop-day) |

The real deadlines come from the host conference's workshop chairs and change each year. The 2027 dates are in [Timeline](chapters/timeline) as an example.

## What is in the guide

- **Chapters** cover one job each: what to do, when, and what went wrong before.
- **Templates** hold the emails we sent, with placeholders for names and dates.
- **[Lessons learned](chapters/lessons-learned)** lists every mistake from 2026 and 2027 and its fix.
