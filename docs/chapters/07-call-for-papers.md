The call goes out twice: a first call about two months before the deadline and a second call a month later. 2027 sent them on 13 October and 13 November 2026.

## What the call states

- Edition, host conference, city and dates.
- Theme, with one paragraph on why it matters this year.
- Topics.
- Submission types: papers (4–8 pages, archival or not), extended abstracts (up to 2 pages, non-archival), ARR commitments.
- Key dates, marked AoE.
- Submission link. Write "to be announced" until the OpenReview venue is live.
- Website and contact address.

## Two versions

Write a short version for social media and a long version for the website and mailing lists. Both are in [CFP email](../templates/cfp-email).

## Where to send it

- The workshop Google Group, as the To address.
- Bcc: `masakhane-nlp@googlegroups.com` and `mlds-africa@googlegroups.com`.
- Masakhane Slack.
- X, LinkedIn and Bluesky.
- Targeted invitations, such as AIMS students. See the template.

## When the submission link goes live

1. Put the OpenReview link on the call page.
2. Add OpenReview's profile note: "New OpenReview profiles created without an institutional email go through moderation, which can take up to two weeks. Please create your profile early."
3. Send a short announcement that submissions are open.
