---
title: "13. Sponsorship"
---

Sponsorship pays for travel grants and registration waivers for African authors. Many authors cannot attend without it.

## Asking

1. Update last year's sponsorship deck: tiers, benefits, last year's numbers.
2. Write to each sponsor with a short, specific ask. See [Sponsorship request](../templates/sponsorship-request).
3. Give evidence: papers accepted, attendees, photos from last year.
4. Start as soon as the proposal is accepted. Company budgets close early.

## Giving grants

- Open an author sponsorship form after notification. One author per accepted paper.
- 2027 closed the form on 16 February.
- Decide awards before the host's early registration deadline, so authors can register at the lower rate.

## Fees to know

- Every presenter must register.
- Authors of non-archival papers and shared task papers do not pay the paper fee.
- The workshop gets one complimentary registration, usually for a speaker.
