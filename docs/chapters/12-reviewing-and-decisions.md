title: "12. Reviewing and decisions"
---

The window between the deadline and notification is three weeks, across the December holidays. Plan every step before the deadline.

## Format check

Right after the deadline, check every submission against the ACL template, the page limit and anonymity. Give non-compliant authors three days to fix the problem. Desk-reject only after that. The call promises this, so keep to it.

## Matching

1. Open the Affinity Score log (Paper Matching Setup).
2. Remove reviewers with no profile or no publications. One reviewer without a profile blocked matching in 2026.
3. Create the assignments, check the loads, then deploy.

Bidding is disabled. There is no time for it.

## Reviews

- Reviewing runs 22 December to 2 January.
- Send a reminder three days before reviews are due, and another on the due date.
- Program chairs write emergency reviews for papers left with fewer than two.

## Decisions

Upload decisions as a CSV with exactly three columns, `paper_number`, `decision`, `comment`, and **no header row**. The 2026 file was rejected four times before we got this right.

Choose oral presentations at the same time. 2026 picked 4 orals from 44 accepted papers.

## Notify authors

OpenReview sends decision emails at Decision Release. To email accepted authors later:

1. Open the venue's Program Chairs console.
2. Under **Venue Roles**, click **Authors (Accepted)**.
3. Search for a paper number, or click **Select All**.
4. Click **Message Selected**. Set the reply-to to the workshop Google Group. Use `{{fullname}}` to personalise.

See OpenReview's guide, [How to email the authors of accepted papers](https://docs.openreview.net/how-to-guides/communication/how-to-email-the-authors-of-accepted-papers).
