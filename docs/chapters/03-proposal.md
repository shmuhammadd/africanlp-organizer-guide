---
title: "3. The proposal"
---

The workshop exists only if the host conference accepts a proposal. \*ACL conferences issue a joint call for workshop proposals, usually 9–12 months before the conference. For 2027 it was the EACL 2027 Joint Call for Workshop Proposals.

## Recruit organisers

1. Ask last year's organisers who wants to continue.
2. Fill gaps with people who can own a job: reviewing, publication, sponsorship, mentorship, communication, the website, a shared task.
3. Create a private Slack channel in the Masakhane workspace, named `africanlp20XX`, and add everyone.

## Write the proposal

1. Copy last year's proposal in Overleaf.
2. Propose a new theme. 2026 was "Multilingual Multimodal LLMs". 2027 is "From Research to Real-World AfricaNLP Applications".
3. Contact candidate speakers and list them. You can add more after acceptance.
4. Update the organiser list, the expected numbers and the format (hybrid, one day).
5. Submit through the host's proposal system.

## What the proposal states

- Theme and topics.
- Aims of the workshop.
- Submission types and page limits.
- Expected submissions and attendance, from last year's numbers.
- Invited speakers and panel.
- Organisers with short bios.
- Reviewing plan: double-blind, OpenReview, ARR commitments accepted.
- Hybrid plan for authors who cannot travel. Visa refusals and travel costs keep many African authors at home.
