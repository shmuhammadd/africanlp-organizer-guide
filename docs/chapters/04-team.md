title: "4. Team and roles"
---

Assign every role at the first meeting after the proposal is accepted. One name per job, even when two people share it, so nothing falls between them.

## Roles

**General chair.** Leads the team and talks to the host conference. Owns the timeline, final approvals and anything nobody else owns.

**Program chairs.** Write the call for papers, recruit reviewers, run OpenReview, make decisions and build the sessions.

**Publication chairs.** Collect camera-ready papers and copyright forms, build the proceedings with aclpub2 and deliver them to the host.

**Sponsorship chair.** Finds sponsors, sends the sponsorship deck, manages travel grants and registration waivers.

**Mentorship chairs.** Pair first-time authors with mentors and run any mentoring session.

**Communication chair.** Owns the mailing lists and the contact address. Sends the calls and the emails to authors, reviewers and speakers.

**Publicity chair.** Runs social media and promotes the calls, speakers and accepted papers.

**Website chairs.** Keep the website correct: call, dates, submission links, programme, accepted papers.

**Shared task chairs.** Fit the shared tasks into the workshop timeline, set common rules, and review system description papers with the publication chairs.

**Task leads (1–2 per task).** Own each task's data, baselines, evaluation, leaderboard and task description paper.

**Award chairs.** Choose the best paper awards.

## Meetings

- Meet every two to four weeks until the deadline, then weekly until decisions.
- Use a when2meet poll for each new slot. Re-poll when the clocks change.
- Keep running notes in one Google Doc in the year's Drive folder, newest meeting at the top.
