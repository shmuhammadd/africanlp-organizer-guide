## The week before

- Confirm every speaker's slot and modality.
- Collect slides from in-person speakers.
- Test the hybrid setup with remote speakers.
- Print certificates and prepare the best paper awards.
- Assign one organiser to run the room and one to run the online session.

## On the day

- Open with a welcome, the theme and the code of conduct.
- Keep time. Each session chair holds a 5-minute card.
- Run posters in person and in online breakout rooms at the same time.
- Close with the awards, thanks to sponsors and reviewers, and next year's plans.

## After

- Post slides and recordings on the website.
- Thank speakers, reviewers and sponsors by email.
- Add your lessons to [Lessons learned](lessons-learned).
- Hand over Drive, Slack, the Google Group and social media to next year's general chair.
