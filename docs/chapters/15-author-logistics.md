---
title: "15. Author logistics"
---

After notification, authors need four things from you: what to record, how posters work, how to get a visa, and how to register. Send one email that covers all four. See [Accepted authors](../templates/accepted-authors).

## Videos and slides

- Every presented paper uploads a video, slides or a poster to Underline, the host's virtual platform.
- Videos are 10 minutes, a strict limit.
- 2027 deadlines: 12 February for pre-recorded videos (hard), 6 March for all materials.
- Send authors the host's Underline instructions when the upload link opens.

## Posters

- In-person and remote posters run at the same time, for one hour.
- Recommend A0 or A1. The host's boards were close to A0 in 2026.

## Visas

Authors register for the host conference to get an invitation letter. The workshop does not issue its own letters. For 2027, point authors to [2027.eacl.org/participants/visa](https://2027.eacl.org/participants/visa/).

Visa refusals are common for African authors. Tell them to apply as early as possible, and remind them that remote presentation is a full part of the programme.

## Registration

- At least one author per accepted paper must register, or the paper leaves the proceedings.
- Authors of non-archival and shared task papers do not pay the paper fee.
- Send a reminder a few weeks before the deadline. See [Registration reminder](../templates/registration-reminder).
