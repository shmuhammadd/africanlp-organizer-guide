title: "1. What AfricaNLP is"
---

AfricaNLP is a one-day workshop on natural language processing for African languages. It has run every year since 2020. Masakhane organises it with volunteers from the African NLP community.

The workshop moved from ICLR to \*ACL conferences after 2024. The organisers now prefer \*ACL hosts. Proceedings go into the ACL Anthology, and ARR-reviewed papers can be committed to the workshop.

## What the workshop accepts

- **Papers**, 4–8 pages. Authors choose archival or non-archival.
- **Extended abstracts**, up to 2 pages. Always non-archival.
- **ARR commitments**: papers with completed ACL Rolling Review reviews and a meta-review.

Page limits exclude references, limitations, ethics sections and appendices. Reviewing is double-blind. Submissions use the ACL template.
