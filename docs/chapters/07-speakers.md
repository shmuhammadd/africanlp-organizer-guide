---

2027 planned two keynotes, one invited talk and a panel. Candidate names go into the proposal. Firm invitations go out after acceptance.

## Choosing speakers

- Match the theme. The 2027 theme is research reaching real users, so the list mixes researchers with founders and lab directors who ship products in African languages.
- Balance academia and industry, regions and languages.
- Ask whether each speaker will come in person. Book at least one strong in-person keynote.
- Keep a shared table: name, affiliation, slot, status (to contact, invited, accepted, declined), modality, and whether they need sponsorship.

## Invitation steps

1. Send the first invitation. Name the theme, the date range and the talk length.
2. On acceptance, send the details request in [Speaker invitation](../templates/speaker-invitation). It asks for slot preference, in person or virtual, sponsorship needs, title, abstract, bio and headshot.
3. 2027 sent detail requests on 1 December with replies due 5 January.
4. Put each speaker on the website as soon as they confirm.

## Talk lengths

- Keynotes and invited talks: 40 minutes, 35 to talk and 5 for questions.
- Panel: 30 minutes with a moderator.

## Registration

Each workshop gets **one** complimentary registration from the host. Every other speaker must register. Tell speakers early. If the sponsorship budget allows, cover registration for the others.
