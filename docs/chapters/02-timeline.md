The deadlines change every year. They depend on the conference that hosts the workshop that year, and the host's workshop chairs set them. They send the dates for submission, ARR commitment, notification, camera-ready and proceedings once the workshop proposal is accepted. Take those dates as given, then work backwards from them for everything else.

The table below shows 2027 as an example. Do not reuse its dates.

## Rules of thumb

- Request the OpenReview venue at least 4 weeks before the direct deadline.
- Invite reviewers the day the venue goes live.
- Put deadlines on Monday to Thursday. OpenReview staff work US Eastern business hours, Monday to Friday.
- Anything that needs OpenReview staff, such as releasing ARR reviews, must go in before the December holidays.
- An AoE deadline at the end of day X is X+1 at 12:00 GMT in OpenReview.
- Use "TBA" on the website for any date not yet fixed.
